Option A is consistent with the CV and reads cleanly, but wastes 169 characters of searchable
space. On a CV that space does not exist; on LinkedIn it is free.

Option B keeps the identity first and adds the four terms recruiters actually filter on for the
target roles — LLM, evaluation/observability, Python, ML — plus remote availability. This is the
recommendation.

Option C adds regulated fintech and payments, which is her deepest domain and genuinely rare.
The risk is that it reads as stuffed, and that fintech keywords pull in fintech-backend roles
she is trying to move away from rather than the AI roles she wants.

## Note on the identity line

"Lead Software Architect" is the noun she chose deliberately: contractors are granted no title,
so she supplies it. It also reads correctly in startup convention, where the lead architect is
the person who owns architecture rather than someone managing a department of architects — and
the profile establishes the startup context immediately.
