HomeServe Finance is HomeServe's in-house consumer-lending business. I moved across from Updraft with the group that stood up its engineering function.

Consumer-credit platform — engineering lead, Loan Management & Payments
Principal contributor to the platform's loan-servicing and collection core for a regulated UK lender: Python/Django on AWS Lambda and Terraform, event-driven integration over SQS/EventBridge, direct-debit collections, loan servicing, arrears and fee logic, early-settlement calculations against UK consumer-credit legislation. Implemented direct-debit integrations with both Stripe and GoCardless, through to decommissioning the superseded payment service. First responder on payment and collection incidents; re-architect for fragile releases.

Grew the team from one engineer to seven in ten months, onboarding or training eleven engineers — several reassigned to other teams once productive. I recruited the in-house hires and onboarded everyone, in-house and outsourced. Moved the core lending service from single-author to mandatory reciprocal review, designed the end-to-end API test framework and taught a team with no prior E2E experience to use it, and wired test failures into alerting so no non-green build shipped.

Python · Django · AWS (Lambda, SQS, EventBridge) · Terraform · PostgreSQL · pytest · Stripe · GoCardless
