We are looking for our first engineer. I am the founder and I run the commercial side; the product
exists today as a set of workflows I operate by hand, and I need someone to turn them into software
that runs without me.

You will own all of it. Architecture, infrastructure, the data model, deployment, monitoring, and
the decisions about what gets built next. There is no engineering team to inherit and no legacy to
work around — you will set every convention yourself, and nobody will tell you how to do it.

The work is production LLM and agent systems: tool-calling pipelines, retrieval over our own
documents, structured outputs, and the guardrails that keep them from doing something expensive
while nobody is watching. We want it reliable enough that the business depends on it.

You will report to me directly and we will talk every day. I will give you the business context and
set the priorities, and you decide everything about how it is built. Equity is available for the
right person, and you would be employee number four.

This suits someone senior who is tired of asking permission.
