Our research group is hiring a scientist to work on the training and adaptation of language models.
You will design experiments, run ablations at scale, and publish results at the main venues. Recent
work from the group covers pre-training data curation, preference optimisation, and the evaluation
of long-context behaviour.

The role is research-first. Engineering quality matters insofar as experiments must be reproducible,
but the deliverable is a finding rather than a system, and the timeline is a conference deadline
rather than a release.

We expect a PhD in machine learning, natural language processing or a closely related field, a
publication record at ACL, EMNLP, NeurIPS or ICLR, and fluency with transformer architectures and
the training stack around them. Experience fine-tuning encoder models for classification tasks and
familiarity with benchmark design are both directly relevant.

You will be supervised by a senior researcher and will collaborate with a platform team that owns
the training infrastructure.
