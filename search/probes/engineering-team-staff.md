We are hiring a staff engineer into a platform group of nine, inside an engineering organisation of
about sixty. The group owns the agent runtime that the product teams build on: tool-calling,
retrieval, evaluation harnesses, and the deployment path that gets a change from a branch to
production behind a flag.

You would work alongside engineers who have been doing this longer than most. Two of them have
spent years on distributed systems and will know more than you about failure modes; one came from
information retrieval and will know more about ranking. Design documents are read and argued over
before anything is built. Every change is reviewed, including yours, and including ours by you.

The staff remit here is technical: setting direction across the group's services, being the person
who decides how a hard problem gets approached, and raising the standard of what the team ships.
It is not a management track and there are no reports.

Current work includes replacing a hand-rolled eval suite with something that scores agent decisions
in production rather than offline, and reducing the cost of a retrieval path that has grown by
accretion. Both are open problems and both are argued about weekly.

Remote within Europe. We expect disagreement in writing and we expect it to be resolved with
evidence.
