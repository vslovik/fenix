Seed-stage company, small team, first engineering hire after the founders. You will build the first
production system from zero: choose the architecture, set up the infrastructure, write the code that
the next ten engineers will inherit, and be in the room where the product decisions are made rather
than receiving them as tickets.

The early work is broad by necessity — data model, API, deployment pipeline, the first customer
integration — and narrows as the team grows. We expect you to have opinions about what to build
properly now and what to leave deliberately rough until it earns the investment.

This suits someone who has done it before: taken a system from a problem statement to something
customers depend on, and lived with the consequences of the early choices. Salary plus meaningful
equity. Remote, with the team distributed across European and US timezones.

You will not be managing anyone in the first year. This is a hands-on role for a senior engineer who
wants ownership rather than headcount.
