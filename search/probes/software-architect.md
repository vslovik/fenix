We need an architect to take ownership of a platform that grew faster than its design. You will
define service boundaries, lead the decomposition of the parts that have become difficult to change,
and set the release and testing discipline that keeps the next year of growth from repeating the
last one.

The role is hands-on. You will write code, run incident response when something breaks, and work
alongside the teams rather than producing documents for them. Expect to spend the first months
building an accurate picture of how the system actually behaves before proposing how it should.

Our environment is Python and PostgreSQL on AWS, with infrastructure as code and a mix of
synchronous APIs and event-driven workers. Domain complexity is real and regulated, so correctness
matters more than throughput.

We are looking for someone senior enough to say no to work, and experienced enough to explain the
trade-off to a non-technical stakeholder without flattening it.
